The impressive part of an AI demonstration is usually visible in the first five minutes. The important part begins afterward.

Operational AI depends on quiet work: permissions, data quality, evaluation, fallbacks, ownership, training, and the ability to understand what happened after a decision. None of these produces the same instant reaction as a polished demo. Together, they determine whether the system survives contact with daily work.

## From novelty to infrastructure

The transition happens when a team can answer ordinary questions with confidence. Who monitors it? What happens when a source changes? Which decisions require review? How do we retire a behaviour that is no longer useful?

That is when AI stops being an event and becomes infrastructure. The technology is still important, but reliability becomes the real product.
